How it works

01

Pick an exam

Type examshell in the terminal, choose exam00 / 01 / 02 / final, then a difficulty. The clock starts immediately — 4h for exam00–02, 8h for final.

02

Write it by hand

Every exercise gives you a subject on the right and an empty .c file on the left. Paste and drag-drop are blocked — client and server side.

03

Submit & get graded

Run grademe (or Ctrl+Enter). Your code compiles with -Wall -Wextra -Werror in a sandbox, runs against the expected output, and comes back OK or KO.

Exams, levels & points

exam00/01/02 each have 10 levels with 2 random exercises per level drawn from a shared pool of 116 — clear both to advance. final has its own fixed 42-exercise curriculum across 15 levels; each attempt still assigns 2 random exercises from that level's list. Points land per level cleared, graded out of 100 like a real piscine exam.

final mixed review · 42 exercises 8h 15 6 pts 100 pts*

Your live score (earned / total) sits in the header once an exam is running, and in the status command. exam00/01/02 levels 5+ pull harder exercises depending on difficulty; final's list is the same regardless.
* final awards 6 pts on each of its 15 levels (90 pts) plus a 10 pt completion bonus on the last one — a full clear lands on exactly 100, same as every other exam.

Difficulty

Applies to exam00/01/02 only — final's curriculum is fixed.

Normal

Classic pacing across all 10 levels — the baseline experience.

Hard

Tougher exercises earlier — same levels, same scoring, less room to warm up.

Extreme

Densest exercise pools, hard problems from the early levels on.

Rules

  • Type it yourself. Paste and file-drop are blocked in the editor — caught client and server side.
  • Allowed functions only. Each exercise lists what you may call; anything else is a KO.
  • No main. The grader links its own — your file holds the required function(s) and nothing more.
  • Crash / timeout / wrong output ⇒ KO. Exact stdout match against the expected output, in an isolated sandbox.
  • Clear 2 per level. Both must pass before the next unlocks — for final, those 2 come at random from that level's fixed list.
